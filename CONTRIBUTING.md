# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve ZShell. It is maintained by one person and is source-available rather than open source (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)), so pull requests are generally not accepted. Please open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Bokjan/ZShell/issues) instead, and discuss a change there first:

- **Bug reports**: the ZShell version (Settings › About), your operating system, the kind of session (SSH, Telnet, serial or local terminal), what you did, what you expected and what happened. Leave out passwords, private keys and host names you'd rather not share.
- **Feature requests**: what you're trying to do and how you do it today, for example in Xshell or `ssh`.

The maintainer implements accepted issues, and thanks the people whose issues lead to a change in the changelog and in [THANKS.md](THANKS.md). Anything you post in an issue may be used in ZShell, as [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) describes under "Feedback and contributions".
